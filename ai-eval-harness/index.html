The Problem This Solves

🚫

No Gate Between Build and Deploy

Teams make informal judgments. No recorded evidence that a method was evaluated before it reached production.

👻

Silent Regressions

A prompt edit or model upgrade silently degrades quality. No mechanism detects it before users do.

📋

No Audit Trail

When something goes wrong, there is no record of what was evaluated, against what criteria, by what process, and who signed off.

🏗️

Ground Truth Bottleneck

Frameworks requiring test datasets before they function are ignored in practice. Most teams cannot invest the upfront effort.

Core Design Principles

01

Method-Centric, Not Model-Centric

The unit of governance is the AI method — a versioned configuration of model + retriever + prompt + graph topology. A model upgrade is a method change and triggers scoped re-evaluation.

02

Works Without Ground Truth

L1 and L2 evaluation fire from day one. L3 activates when a verified golden dataset exists. No use case is blocked from evaluation by the absence of ground truth.

03

Evidence-First Verdicts

A verdict is not a score. It is an immutable record: inputs, outputs, scores
